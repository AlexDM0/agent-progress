# 004 — Account pages dark mode

## Report

Customers browsing at night land on a white account area after a dark storefront. Several asked for it
in the feedback form this month.

## Wanted

The account pages — overview, orders, addresses, payment methods — follow the system colour scheme,
with a manual override that is remembered per browser.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every account page follows `prefers-color-scheme`, with no flash of the wrong theme on load.
- [ ] The override (Auto · Light · Dark) persists across a reload.
- [ ] Every text and control colour meets WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
- [ ] Screenshots of all four pages in both themes, in the evidence folder.

## Brief

Work belongs in: `src/account/**`, `src/theme/tokens.css`, `src/theme/ThemeSwitch.tsx` (new).

Out of bounds regardless: `src/checkout/`, the storefront's own theme.

Facts you do not need to look up:

1. Colours are CSS custom properties in `src/theme/tokens.css`; no component hard-codes a hex value.
2. The design team's dark palette is `docs/design/dark-tokens.json`.

Open exactly these, in one message: `src/theme/tokens.css`, `src/account/Layout.tsx`.

Fails review if false: contrast measured on all four pages in both themes; no flash on a cold load.
