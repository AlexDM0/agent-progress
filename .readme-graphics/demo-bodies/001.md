# 001 — Gift card ignored

## Report

A customer paid €84.90 by card for a basket of €84.90 while holding a €25.00 gift card that the
checkout page showed as applied. The order confirmation lists the gift card, the charge does not.

## Wanted

The amount charged is the basket total minus the applied gift-card balance, never below zero, and the
confirmation shows both figures.

## Acceptance

- A €84.90 basket with a €25.00 card charges €59.90; with a €100.00 card it charges €0.00 and keeps €15.10 on the card.
- The confirmation e-mail and page list the card's share and the charged share.
- `bun test src/checkout` passes, including a spec for the zero-charge case.

## Brief

Work belongs in: `src/checkout/Total.ts`, `src/checkout/Total.spec.ts`, `src/mail/OrderConfirmation.tsx`.

Out of bounds regardless: `src/payments/` — the provider client is shared with subscriptions.

Facts you do not need to look up:

1. Amounts are integer cents everywhere; `Money.subtract` clamps at zero only when asked to.
2. The applied card lives on `basket.giftCard`, not on the order, until payment succeeds.

Fails review if false: the three amounts above, measured in the spec, not by reading the code.
